# Notes:
# - Filename-only matching means two different repos with a same-named file
#   (rare, but possible - e.g. a generic "Math.sol") could false-match. If a
#   CANONICAL result looks suspicious, open the diff by hand once - this
#   heuristic is meant to eliminate 90% of skim-reading, not to be the final
#   word on 100% of files.
# - CUSTOM_OR_MODIFIED does not mean malicious or low quality; it means inspect
#   the diff. EXACT_UPSTREAM does not mean out of scope; still inspect control,
#   initialization, configuration, assets, callbacks, and integration behavior.
# - The script reports the cached upstream commit. Record it in recon evidence.
